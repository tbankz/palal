Welcome to Palal
Palal is the Hebrew word for prayer — to intercede, to entreat, to bring before God.

This is your sacred space to pray, to listen, and to stand in the gap.

Choose Your Mode

Petition · Lament · Thanksgiving · Adoration · Confession

Each mode shapes the prayer and the scripture that meets you.
Hold to Enter

You'll enter the Sanctuary — a space of still prayer with rotating scripture.
